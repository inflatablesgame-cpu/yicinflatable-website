What inflatable products does YIC manufacture?+

YIC manufactures bounce houses, combo units, inflatable water slides, obstacle courses, theme parks, inflatable water parks, inflatable slides, sports games, mechanical rides/games, inflatable tents and advertising inflatables.

Can YIC create custom inflatable designs?+

Yes. We provide OEM and ODM support, professional 3D modeling, custom artwork and production for commercial projects.

What standards and materials do YIC inflatables use?+

We use standard 0.55mm UV-resistant PVC tarpaulin and supply products certified to EN14960, ASTM and CE standards.

Where does YIC ship inflatable products?+

We serve commercial buyers in 57 countries, including the USA, UK and EU, with global logistics support.

What is the minimum order quantity?+

The standard MOQ is 1 unit. You can start with one custom inflatable and scale into a coordinated commercial fleet.

Can product dimensions and colors be customized?+

Yes. Dimensions, colors, artwork, themes, logos and functional layouts can be customized to your site and brand requirements.

How long does OEM production take?+

Lead time depends on product complexity, quantity, artwork approval and the production schedule. YIC confirms a project-specific timeline before order confirmation.

What information is needed for a quotation?+

Send the product type, quantity, target dimensions, destination country, artwork or theme, target delivery date and any required compliance documents.

How should commercial inflatables be maintained?+

Keep the PVC clean and dry, inspect seams and anchor points before use, follow the operating instructions and store the deflated unit in a dry protected place.

Can YIC support inflatable park or resort projects?+

Yes. We can develop custom attraction concepts, modular inflatable parks, water-play formats and coordinated product mixes for commercial sites.
