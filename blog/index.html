Buyer questions

Before you
place an order.

Short answers to the questions commercial buyers often ask before choosing a product or factory.

How long can a commercial inflatable last?

Service life depends on PVC quality, UV exposure, operating frequency, cleaning, storage and repair. Compare the material, reinforcement, workmanship and after-sales support together instead of relying on one headline number.

What should I ask an inflatable manufacturer?

Ask about material specification, design approval, safety documents, quality inspection, packing, lead time, replacement parts, warranty terms and experience in your destination market.

Can YIC make custom inflatables?

Yes. YIC supports OEM and ODM projects with custom artwork, colors, layouts and dimensions for rental operators, FECs, event companies, resorts and distributors.

Where can I ask about a product for my business?

Send the product link, target market, quantity and customization idea through the quote form. You can also contact YIC on WhatsApp.
