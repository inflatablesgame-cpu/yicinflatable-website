How does YIC control inflatable product quality?+

YIC quality control covers material review, digital cutting, sewing, welding or sealing, reinforcement, inflation testing, finishing, packing and photo confirmation. The stated production pass rate is 99.2%, with checks arranged before shipment.

What PVC material thickness does YIC use?+

Many commercial inflatable products use 0.55mm UV-resistant PVC tarpaulin. Some TPU or 0.6mm PVC requirements can be reviewed by product type, market standard, usage intensity and buyer specification.

YIC can provide EN14960, ASTM and CE related documents according to the final product configuration and destination market. Buyers should confirm required standards before production so documentation can be reviewed with the project.

Do you provide repair kits and spare parts?+

Yes. YIC normally provides a repair kit and packing support with commercial inflatable products. Spare parts, blowers, air pumps, bags and additional accessories can be discussed based on the final configuration.

MOQ & Production FAQ

Order planning
without guesswork.

What is YIC minimum order quantity?+

YIC accepts orders from 1 unit. MOQ 1 is useful for sample units, new rental categories, distributor testing, venue pilot projects and custom product development before larger repeat orders.

How long does inflatable production take?+

Production time depends on product complexity, artwork approval, quantity, season and material preparation. Many standard custom products can be planned around a 7-10 work day production reference after design confirmation, while large projects require separate scheduling.

What information should I send for an accurate quotation?+

Send the product idea or link, target size, artwork or logo, quantity, destination country, usage scenario and any certification or material requirement. This helps YIC quote the correct structure, accessories, packing and shipping route.

What payment methods do you accept?+

Payment terms can be confirmed by order and buyer relationship. For custom manufacturing, YIC usually confirms deposit, balance, production schedule and shipment arrangement clearly before production starts.

Can I order one sample unit before a larger order?+

Yes. Many commercial buyers start with one sample unit to verify design, quality, local demand and operating feedback before adding more products to a rental fleet, FEC or distribution catalog.
