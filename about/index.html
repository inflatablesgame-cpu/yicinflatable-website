Our story

Manufacturing
with meaning.

The best inflatable is not just the one that looks exciting on opening day. It is the one that keeps attracting visitors, stays dependable through busy seasons and makes your next investment easier to justify.

That is the standard we work toward at YIC. Since 2010, we have combined practical manufacturing discipline with a deep understanding of how commercial play spaces operate.

We manufacture commercial inflatable equipment including bounce houses, inflatable water slides, obstacle courses and custom attractions for rental businesses, FECs, resorts, distributors and entertainment operators.

Today our 116-person team supports 3,100 brands across 57 countries with OEM and ODM products, custom 3D modeling, multi-stage quality control and global logistics coordination.

Production process / quality control

From drawing
to ready ship.

Every YIC inflatable moves through a defined production route that connects design approval with controlled manufacturing. The workflow is designed to keep the approved artwork, dimensions and functional details clear before production begins.

Drawing, 3D design, pattern making, printing, material cutting, sewing, inspection, packing and dispatch are connected in one practical process for commercial inflatable orders.
