What are mechanical rides and games?+

They are active attractions built around movement, balance, timing or competition, often paired with an inflatable safety arena or interactive equipment.

Can YIC supply the inflatable arena and equipment together?+

YIC can discuss a complete package according to the selected attraction, equipment, controls, accessories and destination requirements.

What information is needed for a mechanical-game quote?+

Provide the game type, equipment reference, capacity, users, floor area, power, venue, destination and opening date.

Are these products suitable for FECs and events?+

They are commonly considered for FECs, fairs, resorts, event companies, amusement venues and distributors.

Can the arena be branded?+

Yes. Arena size, colors, artwork, logos, padding and selected layout details can be discussed during design.

Can documents and service requirements be confirmed?+

Yes. Equipment, material, operating and destination-market documentation should be confirmed for the final package.
