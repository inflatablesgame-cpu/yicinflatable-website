What can an inflatable tent be used for?+

Inflatable tents can serve as booths, entrances, hospitality spaces, registration points, sports shelters, displays and branded event rooms.

Can the tent be printed with our branding?+

Yes. Colors, logos, printed panels, entrances, windows, counters and internal dividers can be customized.

Are inflatable tents suitable for outdoor events?+

They can be designed for the intended environment, but anchoring, weather limits, flooring and local event requirements must be confirmed.

What details should I send for a quote?+

Provide footprint, height, use, location, schedule, floor type, brand assets, quantity and storage expectations.

Can I order one tent?+

Yes. MOQ 1 is available for custom inflatable tent projects.

Will YIC confirm the blower and packing plan?+

Yes. Blower, anchoring, packing and shipping details are confirmed for the approved design and destination.
