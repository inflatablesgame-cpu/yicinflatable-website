What is a bounce house combo?+

A bounce house combo is a multi-activity inflatable that combines a bouncing area with features such as a climbing section, obstacle elements or a slide. The final route and dimensions are customized to the approved design.

Can I order a custom bounce house combo from YIC?+

Yes. YIC can discuss custom size, colors, artwork, branding, entrance layout, slide position and internal play features. A 3D design is reviewed before production.

What is the MOQ for a bounce house combo?+

The minimum order quantity is 1 unit. This suits rental businesses, distributors and venues that want to test a new theme or format before ordering a larger fleet.

What material is used for commercial bounce house combos?+

YIC normally uses 0.55mm UV-resistant PVC tarpaulin for commercial inflatable production. Reinforcement and construction details are confirmed for the final product and application.

How should I choose a combo for my venue?+

Start with the available footprint, access and storage route, then consider the age group, supervision plan, activity mix, theme and shipping requirements. Send these details to YIC for a project-specific recommendation.

Can YIC provide safety and compliance documents?+

YIC can provide relevant EN14960, ASTM and CE documentation according to the final product configuration and destination market. Document scope should be confirmed during quotation.
