What is an inflatable water park?+

It is a connected system of inflatable water-play modules arranged on a pool, lake or designated aquatic area.

Can the water-park layout be expanded later?+

Modular planning can allow staged expansion, subject to the site, connection, anchoring and operating requirements.

What site details does YIC need?+

Provide water body size and depth, surface information, access, anchoring points, supervision plan, user group and operating season.

Can I order one water-park module?+

Yes. Individual modules can be discussed from MOQ 1; a complete system is scoped according to the site and project brief.

How are materials selected?+

Material and reinforcement details are selected for the final water-play design and environment, including seams, handles and anchor points.

Can YIC provide project documents?+

Relevant product, material and component documents can be discussed according to the final configuration and destination market.
