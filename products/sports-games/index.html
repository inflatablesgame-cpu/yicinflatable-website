What kinds of inflatable sports games can YIC make?+

YIC can discuss formats such as football games, target games, boxing arenas, bungee runs, bumper games and other custom interactive concepts.

Can the material be customized?+

Material requirements can be reviewed by product. PVC, TPU or other specifications should be confirmed against the game, impact areas and final application.

Are sports games suitable for rental fleets?+

Yes. Their short-session format works well for events, party rentals, FECs, schools, resorts and brand activations.

Can the game include branding and scoring?+

Yes. Colors, logos, target layouts, scoring panels, artwork and selected accessories can be discussed during design.

What is the MOQ?+

YIC supports MOQ 1 for custom inflatable sports products, subject to the confirmed design and accessories.

How do I request an accurate quote?+

Provide the game objective, users, footprint, material preference, accessories, quantity, destination and delivery timing.
