What is the difference between a bounce house and a combo?+

A bounce house focuses on enclosed jumping, while a combo adds connected activities such as a slide, climbing section or obstacle route. YIC can help compare both formats for your site and rental model.

Are YIC bounce houses available with custom artwork?+

Yes. Colors, logos, printed panels, themes, dimensions and selected layout details can be customized after the design brief is reviewed.

Can I buy one bounce house for a rental business?+

Yes. YIC supports MOQ 1, which lets a rental operator test a format or add a distinctive theme without committing to a large quantity.

What material do you use for bounce houses?+

The standard commercial material is 0.55mm UV-resistant PVC tarpaulin. Construction and reinforcement details are confirmed for each final design.

Where can a bounce house be used?+

Typical applications include party rentals, FECs, indoor play centers, schools, resorts, community events and branded activations, subject to local operating requirements.

Can YIC provide compliance documents?+

Relevant EN14960, ASTM and CE documents can be discussed according to the final product configuration and destination market.
