An inflatable water slide is a water-play attraction designed around an elevated climbing route and one or more sliding lanes. The landing area may be a splash pool or a connection to an existing water feature, depending on the approved design. Selection must account for water management, site drainage, supervision, user flow and local requirements.

What should I confirm before buying an inflatable water slide?+

Confirm the site footprint, water source, drainage, landing area, supervision position, user group, access route and destination-market requirements before selecting a design.

Can YIC customize the lane layout and pool?+

Yes. Lane count, slide route, pool or landing arrangement, colors, artwork and overall dimensions can be discussed during the design stage.

Is the MOQ for an inflatable water slide one unit?+

Yes. YIC supports MOQ 1, subject to confirming the final product and shipping requirements.

What material is used for water slides?+

YIC normally specifies commercial PVC tarpaulin with reinforcement and construction details matched to the final water-play design.

Who buys inflatable water slides?+

Typical buyers include resorts, water parks, outdoor rental companies, event companies, camps and distributors.

Can documents be supplied for a water-slide project?+

YIC can discuss relevant product, material and component documents according to the final configuration and destination market.
