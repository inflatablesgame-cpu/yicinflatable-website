An advertising inflatable is a branded air-supported display or interactive structure designed to attract attention in a public or event environment. It can be an inflatable kiosk for product information, an inflatable booth for an activation, an inflatable concession stand for food and drinks, or an inflatable stall for a market or retail event. The right format depends on viewing distance, transport, installation and message priority.

What are advertising inflatables used for?+

They are used for brand activations, roadshows, retail displays, product launches, sports sponsorships, festivals, entrances and event photography. YIC can also develop an inflatable kiosk, booth, concession stand or stall for the campaign brief.

Can YIC make a product replica or mascot-style display?+

Yes. YIC can discuss original branded shapes, product-inspired displays, arches, logos and interactive concepts based on the buyer's brief and rights.

What artwork should I provide?+

Vector logos, brand colors, reference images, product proportions and the intended viewing distance are useful for accurate design.

Can advertising inflatables be used outdoors?+

They can be designed for the intended environment, but anchoring, weather limits, print durability and local event rules must be confirmed.

What is the MOQ?+

MOQ is 1 unit, which supports a single campaign, prototype or repeat branded-display program.

Can YIC provide 3D artwork approval?+

Yes. YIC can develop a 3D direction for review before production, followed by manufacturing, QC and shipping coordination.
