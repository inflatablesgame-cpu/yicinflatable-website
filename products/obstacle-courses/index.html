What is an inflatable obstacle course used for?+

Obstacle courses are used for timed races, team activities, school events, festivals, rental programs, FECs and resort entertainment.

Can the obstacle route be customized?+

Yes. YIC can discuss course length, lanes, obstacles, themes, colors, branding, entrances, exits and slide features, including a customized inflatable military obstacle course brief.

What is the MOQ?+

The minimum order quantity is 1 unit, allowing a buyer to test a new route or event format.

How do I choose the right course size?+

Start with the available footprint, access route, queue area, supervision sightlines, target users and expected throughput.

What material is normally used?+

YIC normally specifies 0.55mm UV-resistant PVC tarpaulin with design-specific reinforcement and construction details.

Can YIC provide OEM design and documents?+

Yes. YIC supports 3D OEM/ODM design and can discuss relevant EN14960, ASTM and CE documents by project.
