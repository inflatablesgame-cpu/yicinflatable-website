What is a standalone inflatable slide?+

It is an inflatable attraction focused on a climbing route and slide exit, without requiring a full bounce house combo.

Can YIC make a custom inflatable slide?+

Yes. Height, lanes, colors, artwork, entrance, slide route and overall footprint can be customized after design review.

Can inflatable slides be used for rental businesses?+

Yes. They are commonly considered for party rentals, events, resorts, schools, FECs and seasonal programs.

What should I measure before ordering?+

Measure the inflated footprint, height, landing clearance, access route, queue space and supervision position.

What material is used?+

YIC normally uses 0.55mm UV-resistant PVC tarpaulin with construction details matched to the approved design.

Is the MOQ one unit?+

Yes. YIC supports MOQ 1 for custom inflatable products, subject to confirming the final design and shipment.
